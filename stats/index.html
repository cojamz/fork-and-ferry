The companionUpper floor · CR 8
Old dwarf battlemaster, undisguised. AC 18 (Order plate and shield), 149 hp, speed 25.
STR 18 DEX 12 CON 18 INT 12 WIS 16 CHA 13. Saves Str +7, Con +7, Wis +6. Skills Athletics +7, Insight +6, Perception +6. Darkvision 60.
Order Warding. Advantage on saves against spells and magical effects. This is his gear, not his blood — strip it, break it or catch him without it and the trait is gone.
Dwarven Resilience. Advantage against poison, resistance to poison damage.
Superiority. Four d10 dice, regained on a short rest. He is old: when they run out the fight gets plainer and worse for him.
Multiattack. Three warhammer attacks, +8 to hit, 1d8+4.
Maneuvers (spend a die, DC 15): Trip (Str save, prone) · Disarm (Str save, drops an item) · Menace (Wis save, frightened) · Push (Str save, 15 feet) · Precision (add the die to the roll). All add the die to damage.
Reactions. Parry, reduce damage by 1d10+4. Riposte, one attack back.
